From the editor’s letter

If you let machines write the code, how do you know when they are cutting corners?

  1. A contract frozen before the work begins.Rizzstack & Workflow · No. 201
  2. A button that is not allowed to mean yes.uirizz · No. 205
  3. A hook that steers a model away from forty turns of gear math by hand.photo-to-part · No. 209
  4. A README that opens with its own failure rate.Aircraft Doppler Geolocation · No. 207
  5. A globe that will find any ship on Earth and refuses, in nineteen lines, to look for a person.Eye Of Hermes (EOH) · No. 208

  6. None of it is about what the machines can do. All of it is about what it takes to trust them.

Corrections & clarifications

We Got These Wrong

A magazine written with machines should be the first to admit what the machines, and the people, got wrong. Here is our list. It will grow.

  1. Issue 01’s Source Room promised to keep your reading list. It kept everyone’s, in one shared pile on the server. Each reader now gets their own.
  2. On the live site, Issue 01’s reading-list endpoint has been answering with a 404. This was not an artistic choice. The Living Edition ships a new backend.
  3. An earlier draft of this issue said photo-to-part ships 15 templates. It ships 16. We counted on our fingers this time.
  4. The same draft filed the 608 bearing under ISO. The plugin checks it against the SKF size. Corrected.
  5. That draft also gave the frontispiece a precise timestamp, 02:14 UTC, which nothing in the record supports. It sounded nice. We took it out.
Dispatches

Also on the Bench

Four more that didn’t get a full feature. No reflection on their character.
@f5585b9 ↗

Comfyrizz: a film studio you talk to ↗

Type a brief, approve a short proposal, get a narrated film back. A Pi agent plans it, ComfyUI on Google Colab renders FLUX 3 Klein stills and LTX-2.5 clips, and code draws every word on screen, so the image model is never asked to spell. The demo film, Mira’s Light, cost 2.67 Colab compute units.

@dee2732 ↗

Shortrizz: long video in, shorts out ↗

Long video in, vertical shorts out. Whisper on the CPU, a face tracker that follows whoever is talking, karaoke captions, and a first frame chosen to double as the thumbnail. The README’s first instruction is to clip only what you have the rights to.

@35c482e ↗

Hyperrizz: frame-accurate notes for HyperFrames ↗

A production discipline for HyperFrames films, with a live review pane. Pause on a frame, write a note, and send it back to Hermes with the exact frame attached. The motto is printed in the README: reuse the engineering, invent the film.

@51c12d0 ↗

StitchSlop for Hermes: embroidery from chat ↗

Drive the Stitch Slop embroidery editor from chat and export DST or PES files for a real machine. The pairing token is pasted by a human, by hand, because the agent is not allowed to write that file.
