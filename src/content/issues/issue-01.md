Eleven projects from **SLOPTIMUS PRIME**, illustrated and explained. Every monograph separates what a repository actually contributes from the upstream foundations it stands on—whether that means putting a supervised Pi-agent helper behind Warp's native terminal UI in **Warpi**, bolting a lock-free Musical Clock onto Guitar Companion in **JamMate**, or admitting up front that **Becomussy**'s search is keyword-based while vector drift detection stays on the drawing board.

Follow the commit-pinned source links or open *Under the hood* for the implementation details, verified capabilities, and exact line-numbered excerpts. Conceptual Blender still lifes and interactive SVG studies are labelled as editorial illustrations; product captures and mock fixtures state their provenance plainly in the caption.
