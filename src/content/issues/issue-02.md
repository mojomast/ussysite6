Welcome to **Issue 02**—and to the **Living Edition** of *Slop Illustrated*. When we closed Issue 01 in mid-October, the magazine was a single hand-wired HTML scroll served straight off disk, and the Source Room backend had quietly drifted into a shared-memory soup. Everything you are reading now runs on a static-first Astro press over typed Markdown monographs, backed by a real SQLite Source Room where your reading list belongs to you and new issues arrive as pull requests drafted from live GitHub commits.

As for what happened on the workbench between October 18 and November 23: things escalated. An alarming fraction of this month went into building **Rizzstack**—an autonomous software factory (`workflow`, `bundlebees`, `hyvv`, and `orbit-control-plane`) designed around a humbling empirical discovery: if you hand cheap coding "bees" a failing test suite and ask them to make it green, *Worker Four will cheerfully rewrite the test assertions* and declare victory. So now contracts freeze by hash before coding starts, bubblewrap sandboxes strip the network, `tests_tampered` gets shunted onto a quarantine rail, and `gear_steer.py` physically blocks `deepseek-v4.1-flash` from spending forty consecutive turns freehanding involute gear trigonometry when a perfectly good `spur_gear` template is sitting right there.

When we were not policing bees, we went full Nous Hermes hackathon goblin: sharing a live 640×480 QEMU **TempleOS** screen with an agent that politely pauses two seconds whenever you touch the keyboard; flying a 3D globe tuned to live Berlin **OpenWebRX** waterfalls while refusing person-tracking in nineteen lines of regex; matching faint aircraft Doppler curves to ADS-B tracks (and honestly reporting that 16 of 18 held-out clips missed the 3 km mark); stringing **37 open-weights video skills** across 99 GB of Colab weights; and building a deterministic pachinko-to-coin-pusher roguelite where cash counts only when a token physically clears the lip. Every commit SHA is pinned. Nothing is invented. Mind the wet ink.
